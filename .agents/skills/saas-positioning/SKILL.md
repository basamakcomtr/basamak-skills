# SaaS Positioning

## Goal
Help a SaaS team answer **who it serves, which urgent problem it solves, why an alternative is insufficient, and what evidence makes the promise credible**. Positioning is a decision, not a collection of slogans.

## Process
1. Gather only available facts: product capabilities, customer segment, buying trigger, alternatives, pricing model, current wording, and real proof. Distinguish shipped features from roadmap promises.
2. Identify at most **two credible primary segments**. For each, name the job-to-be-done, trigger, desired result, objections, and reachability. Do not label every possible buyer as the target.
3. Compare the product against the customer's actual alternatives: manual work, spreadsheets, existing software, outsourcing or doing nothing. Mention specific competitor claims only when verified with dated sources.
4. Use a **positioning decision table**: audience, painful situation, unique approach, concrete benefit, evidence, unresolved assumptions. If evidence is weak, suggest a narrower claim instead of manufacturing a differentiator.
5. Recommend one primary positioning statement plus an alternative only if a material strategic tradeoff exists. Draft homepage headline, one-line explanation, three benefit bullets and a single appropriate CTA without invented metrics.
6. Suggest one fast validation: customer-call question, sales-objection review, onboarding language analysis or small message test. Say which evidence could change the choice.

## Output
A one-paragraph diagnosis; primary segment; alternative being replaced; positioning statement; message hierarchy; proof gaps; a low-effort validation step.

## Quality gates
Is the outcome recognizable to the customer? Could the promise be proven? Could any competitor say the exact same sentence? Remove generic "revolutionary"/"all-in-one" claims unless substantiated. Do not send outreach, change live pages, scrape restricted accounts or imply research you did not do. For conversion issues use `conversion-optimizer`; for pricing strategy use `pricing-review`.
